## ⚡ 3. How to Run the System & Share with Clients (2 Clicks)

1. **Step 1: Start the Local Backend**
   Double-click `start.bat` in the `nexus-agent-system` folder.
   *(Opens `http://localhost:3000`)*

2. **Step 2: Generate Public HTTPS Link for Clients**
   Double-click `share_online.bat` in the `nexus-agent-system` folder.
   *(It will show a public link like `https://quick-sample-tunnel.trycloudflare.com`)*

3. **Step 3: 1-Click Outreach**
   Open your Agency Dashboard (`http://localhost:3000`):
   * Click **`✉️ Outreach Pitch`** on any client card.
   * Click **`Copy WhatsApp Pitch`** or **`Copy Full Cold Email`**.
   * Replace the link with your public Cloudflare URL and send!
